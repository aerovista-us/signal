  Key Learnings & Roadblocks

   * Environmental Incompatibility: The primary roadblock was the severe incompatibility of the development environment (UNC network drive,
     specific GPU setup) with standard Electron development workflows.
   * `npm` and UNC Paths: npm install (especially for Electron) does not reliably function from UNC network paths.
   * Mapped Drive Access: Mapped network drives (e.g., X:) were not consistently accessible within the execution environment, preventing a common
     workaround.
   * Persistent GPU Errors: Electron repeatedly failed to launch its GPU process, even with aggressive --disable-gpu flags, indicating a deeper
     system-level issue.
   * Strategic Pivot: These persistent issues necessitated a pivot from attempting to fix the Electron environment to converting the application
     to a more robust and efficient framework like Tauri.
