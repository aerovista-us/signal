Strategic product shift

From finding problems to helping solve them.

LotScope is increasingly able to test multiple ways to improve a difficult property plan: move a building, reposition a garage, reshape a driveway, increase pavement only where it adds value, and compare the consequences before recommending a direction.

The goal is not a single formula. The goal is the best practical use of the property.

Working direction

Pondy Flats is proving the model

A difficult real-world property is forcing the system to balance living space, parking, setbacks, access, usability, and design quality at the same time.

Design breakthrough

Design #2 clarified a viable direction

A more accurate interpretation of detached-garage placement indicated roughly 20 additional feet of usable design depth. That working analysis allowed the homes to remain connected while shifting garages rearward and improving circulation, subject to final site and design confirmation.

Current focus

Design #4 is testing circulation quality

The current work is testing whether final parking and release maneuvers can be made clean, comfortable, and presentation-ready while retaining four enclosed stalls. The geometry remains under review until it clears the required gates.

Northline · EchoStory → Collection → Commerce

Northline is being rebuilt as a compact attention-capture product, not a sprawling platform.

Northline's current standalone experience presents the Northline Collection as an AeroVista / EchoVerse Audio property built around North Idaho landscape language—cold peaks, dark timber, still water, snow, reflection and hidden apex geometry. The reconstructed experience contains six visible chapters/tracks, with the lead Northline signal available as a demo and the remaining masters intentionally shown as pending rather than playing incorrect audio.

Experience

Six-part collection structure

Northline · Blue Divide · Ridgeline · Idaho After Dark · Powderline · Source Code. The experience already carries a cohesive visual/story language rather than functioning as a generic music player.

Commercial architecture

Centralized checkout path staged

Northline is part of the shared NXCore Commerce direction alongside other AeroVista stores. Its storefront integration is designed to route through the common commerce edge instead of embedding provider-specific payment logic into every niche experience.

Not live commerce yet

Checkout remains deliberately gated

Production checkout should stay disabled until the shared backend, Northline catalog, server-authoritative pricing, payment behavior, webhook correlation and store-level acceptance are proven end to end. This is staged work, not completed revenue activation.

Northline is useful because it demonstrates a model AeroVista can repeat: small branded experience, focused content, products, shared commerce underneath.
The Last Normal Night · Original IP

A ten-track story is becoming a multi-format AeroVista property.

The Last Normal Night has advanced beyond a music concept. The core narrative is structured as ten connected chapters set around a distorted Coeur d'Alene reality, with recurring continuity objects, a defined mythology, a web-player lane and a comic/graphic-story production lane. The September 11 handoff exists specifically to prevent the project from losing canon or restarting from scratch.

Canon established

Ten connected story chapters

The narrative progresses from The Last Normal Night through The Wrong Side of Morning, Frequency Three, The Man in My Shadow, The Lake Remembers, Don't Answer Yourself, I Already Did This, Welcome Back, The Door Was Never for Me and Midnight Knows My Name.

Shared signals—including 11:59, the lake, the truck radio, recurring local landmarks, the wrong shadow and Frequency Three—tie the album together as one story rather than ten unrelated tracks.

Production system documented

Music + web + comic are being treated as one IP system

The project handoff captures the album/story canon, audio status, track art, player work, comic architecture, Coeur d'Alene geography rules, character/object continuity and the Episode 1 production map. That gives future work a trustworthy source instead of reinterpreting the story every session.

Current production lane

Resume from the locked visual sequence

The correct continuation is the established comic workflow beginning at the trusted Page 7 point and continuing forward through the already mapped episode sequence. The work should now prioritize consistent finished pages rather than opening another round of story ideation.

Strategic value

Original IP expands what AeroVista can own

Unlike client or utility work, The Last Normal Night is an owned narrative world that can connect recorded music, artwork, interactive web presentation, serialized comics and future physical/digital products under one property.
