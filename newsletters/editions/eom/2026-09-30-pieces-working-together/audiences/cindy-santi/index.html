What changed
Store → system

The apparel experience now has a working transaction foundation.

Product structure, checkout, pricing, browser access, and fulfillment mapping are connected. That moves the next question away from “can the store sell?” and toward “how well does the brand present itself?”

Brand rule
Customer first

The store should sell the brand—not explain the plumbing.

With the transaction path established, the focus can shift to product quality, imagery, messaging, and the customer journey.

What moved forward

September client-facing progress.

The strongest work this month was the move from storefront concept to a cleaner working commerce flow.

01

Five-product hoodie collection

The Cindy collection is structured around five approved designs with a complete public size matrix.

02

Square purchase flow

The temporary request-order path was replaced by hosted checkout with authoritative $68 product pricing.

03

Print mapping connected

Public product variations were matched to the production fulfillment system so customer choices can travel cleanly from storefront to order processing.

Promotion

Cindy Connect offer

The one-order / one-per-style promotion is defined as $25 off each selected style, up to five styles. It creates a clear launch incentive without turning the brand into a permanent discount store.

Brand expansion

Life Coach + Death Doula lane

Creative work extended the Cindy visual identity into talks and events without making the Death Doula work feel like an unrelated second brand. The October Sandpoint Library event has a dedicated print-ready visual direction.

Where we create the next improvement

Solutions, not internal backlog.

The working commerce foundation should now be protected while the visible customer experience gets stronger.

1 · Artwork
Finish production masters. Preserve canonical artwork so every future product uses the approved source rather than a compressed mockup or derivative.
2 · QC
Review the physical product. Check real garment output, placement, color, scale, and print quality. A polished store can only promise what the physical product can deliver.
3 · Story
Strengthen lifestyle presentation. Build imagery and storytelling that makes the clothing feel like part of Cindy’s message rather than simply branded merchandise.
Opportunity

Connect apparel and coaching without forcing them together.

Use one recognizable Cindy identity with distinct paths: apparel for wearable messages, coaching for personal transformation, and event material for the Life Coach / Death Doula work.

Opportunity

Use events as trust-building moments.

Talks and workshops can bridge naturally to future coaching, content, community, and selected products without turning the event itself into a sales pitch.

Recommended next move

Protect the working store. Improve what the customer sees and receives.

Put the next effort into print-master quality, real-product review, stronger imagery, and a clean connection between Cindy’s apparel and her broader transformational work.
