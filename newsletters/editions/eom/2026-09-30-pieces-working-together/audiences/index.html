Disclosure model

One Signal system. Different disclosure surfaces.

The design stays recognizably AeroVista while the content narrows to what each audience actually needs.

Shareholder

Company-level progress, business meaning, risks, lessons, and next-stage leverage.

Invested Team

Operational gates, incidents, unresolved lineage, acceptance boundaries, and execution priorities.

Client Direct

Only that client’s work, the value created, decisions clarified, and recommended next solutions.

Excluded by default

Unrelated client work, unnecessary infrastructure detail, and internal platform/security material that does not help the recipient.

Current Signal hosting does not yet enforce audience-specific authentication. These pages are separated by communication scope and URL, not by a private-access wall.
