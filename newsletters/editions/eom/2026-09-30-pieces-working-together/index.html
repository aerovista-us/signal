Why it matters

Good governance sometimes looks slower.

A public-site redesign was held back when source provenance could not reproduce the known-good production build. Work Log history was not recreated and passed off as authoritative. New device work was not granted old device acceptance. The short-term cost is less dramatic reporting. The long-term benefit is a company that can trust its own status.

Plain-English desk

No decoder ring required.

A few terms used throughout this edition.

Shared identity

One trusted sign-in and account system that can work across multiple products instead of each product inventing its own.

Runtime acceptance

Proof that the intended version is actually running correctly in the environment where people use it—not merely that the code exists.

Human acceptance

A real user or operator successfully completes the intended workflow. Automated tests cannot substitute for this.

Governance

The rules, ownership, checks, records, and boundaries that keep work secure, recoverable, accountable, and honest about what has actually been proven.

Professional / AHJ boundary

Software can organize evidence and reduce discovery work, but licensed professionals, recorded property facts, and authorities having jurisdiction still control decisions that legally or professionally belong to them.
