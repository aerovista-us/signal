Art Localized production upgrade

Revision-pinned Approve & Launch

Request Review now pins submittedRevisionId. Artists use Save Draft and Request Review; artist Publish is blocked. Operators approve and launch the exact revision, then complete promote, deploy, launch-kit and verification through the workstation command.

This closes the stale “promote latest” risk without pretending Firebase deployment already runs from NXCore.

Creator media upgrade

Gallery add-on shipped

Creators can enable a Gallery, select from the media library and publish up to a soft cap of 12 items through addOns.gallery. Classic booths can now show media beyond the hero.

Original IP

The Lake Knows Better

The finished four-panel strip established a repeatable story engine: the fisherman states a rule, the lake challenges it, chaos follows, and he reframes the result as proof he was right. It can support Vespera publishing, Frontier content, social serialization and merchandise.

Booth and market polish: Vespera’s CTA was corrected, DigiMart gained Gumroad-backed featured products and a commerce override, Cindy and DigiMart were pinned as featured booths, and public placeholder modes were hidden while deeper mode work continues.

Executive Summary

July produced meaningful public-facing activity across Art Localized, Horizon, Vespera, AeroVista’s public site, and EchoVerse creative output.

The strongest market-facing development was Art Localized’s local creator campaign. The strongest visual product development was Horizon’s curated gallery experience. The strongest new creative IP was the Old Fisherman comic-series concept.
