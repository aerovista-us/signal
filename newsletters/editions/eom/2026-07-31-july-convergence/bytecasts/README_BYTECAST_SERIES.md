# AeroVista July 2026 Month-End ByteCast Series

## Series Purpose

A six-episode companion series for the modular July 2026 month-end reports.

The episodes work in order or independently. Each script is written as clean spoken text for browser or TTS playback, without production prompts embedded inside the narration.

## Series Arc

| Episode | Title | Source Report | Target Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | July Convergence | Executive Brief | 7–9 min |
| 01 | The System Holds | Platform, Infrastructure, and Operations | 7–9 min |
| 02 | Identity to Value | Identity, Business Systems, and Commerce | 8–10 min |
| 03 | The Work Itself | Divisions and Product Development | 9–11 min |
| 04 | Into the World | Markets, Media, and Creative Output | 7–9 min |
| 05 | August: The Test | Portfolio Control and August Plan | 7–9 min |

## Voice Direction

Confident female operations storyteller. Calm authority, clear diction, medium pace, emotionally grounded. Internal confidence with external clarity. Avoid corporate hype, feature dumping, or repeated introductions to the divisions.

## Sonic Continuity

Use one shared ByteCast identity across the series, but give each episode a distinct movement.

- **Episode 00:** Wide cinematic digital atmosphere; convergence and momentum.
- **Episode 01:** Low mechanical pulse, subtle routing clicks, restrained system hum.
- **Episode 02:** Precise clockwork rhythm, soft authentication tones, financial pulse.
- **Episode 03:** More organic and varied movement; each division changes texture without becoming a montage.
- **Episode 04:** Warmer public-facing sound, local atmosphere, camera shutters, gallery air, restrained market energy.
- **Episode 05:** Minimal, decisive, forward-driving pulse with a firm close.

## Production Rules

- Do not reintroduce all divisions in every episode.
- Do not repeat the same July headline word-for-word.
- Keep sound underneath the narration.
- Avoid excessive risers, trailer impacts, or generic corporate music.
- Use short transitions only when the subject truly changes.
- Preserve the exact metrics and operational states from the source reports.
- Keep each episode useful without requiring the listener to hear the prior episode.
