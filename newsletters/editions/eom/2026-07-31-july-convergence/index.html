Portfolio control

August is the proof month

July proved capability. August must show durable acceptance, fewer active lanes and one complete commercial journey.

Primary execution risks

  • Too many active lanes weakening closure.
  • Runtime fixes not yet preserved in source.
  • Technical completion without adoption.
  • Identity and permission models still maturing by service.
  • EchoVerse request amplification and user-facing defects.

August operating commitments

  • Stabilize and physically accept EchoVerse.
  • Finish Profit Compass pilot controls.
  • Make AVCC the portfolio authority.
  • Prove one full revenue path.
  • Create the deliverables and asset register.
