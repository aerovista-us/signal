## AeroVista-Made Workspaces

An AeroVista Workspace is a purpose-built digital working environment created by AeroVista for a specific division, team, project, customer, or operational situation.

Each workspace brings together the tools, information, files, people, permissions, tasks, and workflows required to complete a particular type of work.

Instead of forcing every division to operate inside one generic dashboard, AeroVista creates specialized workspaces that match how the work is actually performed.

A Horizon photographer should not have to work inside the same interface as an EchoVerse music operator.

A Summit instructor should not need the same tools as an AVCC finance administrator.

The workspaces can look and behave differently while still connecting to the same AeroCore OS foundation and AVCC operating records.

### What an AeroVista Workspace Contains

Depending on its purpose, a workspace may include:

- Specialized tools and editors
- Project and task information
- Customer or creator records
- Files, images, music, video, documents, and other assets
- Approval and review steps
- Notes, decisions, and activity history
- Team communication and handoffs
- Production status
- Costs, time, usage, and deliverables
- Publishing or deployment controls
- Role-based access and permissions
- Links to related AeroVista services

The workspace is where people perform the work.

AVCC records the commercial and operational evidence created by that work.

AeroCore OS manages the shared platform services, identity, permissions, devices, infrastructure, and connections between systems.

---

## Example 1: Horizon Photography Project Workspace

A Horizon workspace could be created for a commercial property photography project.

The workspace might include:

- Customer and property details
- Shoot date, location, and access instructions
- Weather and flight-planning information
- Drone and camera equipment checklist
- Required photographs and video shots
- FAA or local flight documentation
- Raw image upload area
- Image selection and editing tools
- Customer review gallery
- Revision requests
- Final delivery package
- Print or licensing options

### How it might be used

Before the shoot, the operator reviews the location, shot list, weather, equipment, and customer requirements.

During production, images and flight records are attached to the project.

After the shoot, selected photographs move into the editing and review process.

Once approved, the final files are delivered to the customer.

The workspace reports completed work, equipment use, deliverables, time, costs, and billing information back to AVCC.

The workspace might include:

- Genre, mood, tempo, and instrumentation data
- ByteTag and classification tools

- Playback and listener activity

### How it might be used

EchoVerse analyzes the track and creates an initial metadata record.

An operator reviews the title, artist, album, lyrics, artwork, classifications, and release information.

The track moves through approval and becomes available in the EchoVerse library and Android application.

The workspace records the release, operator activity, deliverables, and associated costs in AVCC.

## Permanent and Temporary Workspaces

Not every workspace needs to be permanent.

### Permanent workspaces

These support ongoing areas of the company.

Examples include:

- EchoVerse Music Operations
- Horizon Production
- Art Localized Creator Management
- Summit Learning Administration
- AVCC Executive Operations
- NXCORE Infrastructure Operations

### Temporary workspaces

These are created for a defined event or project and can later be archived.

Examples include:

- A customer website launch
- A commercial drone shoot
- A music album release
- A security incident
- A product launch
- A hiring and onboarding cycle
- A company audit
- A major server migration

The workspace can be created from a reusable AeroVista template and configured for the specific situation.

---

## How the Platforms Work Together

The relationship can be summarized simply:

**AeroCore OS manages the environment.**

It handles shared identity, permissions, devices, services, infrastructure, assets, and platform connections.

**AeroVista Workspaces are where specialized work happens.**

They provide the interfaces and tools required by a division, project, customer, or operational situation.

**AVCC records how the work affects the business.**

It tracks customers, contacts, projects, tasks, work records, costs, quotes, invoices, payments, profitability, and executive performance.

A person might complete a task inside a Horizon workspace.

The photograph might be stored through AeroCore asset services.

The time, deliverable, customer project, cost, and invoice status would then be recorded in AVCC.

---

## The Main Advantage

The value of AeroVista-made workspaces is not simply that everything is placed on one screen.

The real advantage is that AeroVista can design each environment around the actual work while maintaining one connected operating system underneath.

Teams receive tools that make sense for their role.

Customers see only what they need.

Leadership receives consistent evidence.

Divisions remain specialized.

And the company gains a shared operational record without forcing every type of work into the same generic application.
