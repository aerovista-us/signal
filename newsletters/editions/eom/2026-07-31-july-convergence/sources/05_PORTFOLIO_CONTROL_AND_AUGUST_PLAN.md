# AeroVista Portfolio Control and August Execution Plan

## Executive Summary

July produced significant progress across the portfolio, but AeroVista now needs stronger portfolio control.

The central problem is not lack of work. It is too much simultaneous work, incomplete closure, and inconsistent evidence of adoption.

August should reduce work in progress, assign clear owners, finish the highest-value systems, and prove at least one complete commercial path.

## Portfolio-Control Standard

Every active project should contain:

- Project name.
- Division.
- Owner.
- Current state.
- July progress.
- Evidence.
- Blocker.
- Next action.
- Priority.
- Dependency.
- Commercial relevance.
- Public or internal status.

## Deferred/Paused Register

Projects should be paused deliberately when they lack:

- Owner.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Near-term business value.
- Required dependency.
- Time allocation.

Paused work should remain searchable and documented, not silently abandoned.

## August Definition of Done

A project is not complete because it builds or deploys.

A project is complete only when:

- Source is durable.
- Tests pass.
- Deployment is verified.
- Ownership is clear.
- Documentation is current.
- Support path exists.
- Acceptance criteria are met.
- Evidence is attached.
- Real use or outcome is measured.
- Rollback is documented.

## Closing Direction

August should be a month of closure, acceptance, and proof.

The strongest move is not another broad expansion of AeroVista’s scope.

It is finishing the highest-value systems, showing that people use them, and proving that the connected ecosystem can generate repeatable business outcomes.
