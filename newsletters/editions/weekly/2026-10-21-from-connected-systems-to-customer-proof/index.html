04 / Boundaries

What this does not certify.

Digital design ≠ physical approval

LotScope does not establish structural, drainage, fire access or permit acceptance. AutoBench cannot approve cutting, welding, suspension fitment or roadworthiness from conceptual geometry.

Bag ready ≠ payment accepted

Apparel's UI does not prove a Square redirect, valid variation ID or completed paid order. Scoped releases require authenticated staging, isolated publication and rollback.

Merged ≠ production verified

Passing CI is not a substitute for checking the live hostname, correct deployed commit, authenticated user journey and media asset response.

Browser save ≠ trusted measurement

AutoBench's local saved revision does not create verified physical evidence or cross-device cloud persistence.

Post-period checkpoint — October 23: The supplied AutoBench handoff records PRs #19 and #21 as published and PR #23 as draft. The supplied Apparel handoff records responsive QA on draft PR #26, while Store PR #12 and Apparel PR #25 remain held for authenticated staging and checkout. 05 / Looking ahead

Make proof as useful as the product.

Next, demonstrate complete journeys: choose/save/reload/report the same LotScope design; tie real captured dimensions to AutoBench drawings without forging verification; prove Apparel's authorized scoped publish/read/Square-handoff/rollback sequence; and verify Signal's served HTML and audio from its public domain after deployment.

Across AeroVista, the best customer experience will make state and authority obvious: what was saved, which revision produced an output, why validation passed or failed, and which external approvals are still required.
