01 · What changed

Several gates moved out of WIP.

The most important portfolio improvement this week was not a new application. It was the removal of solved engineering gates from the active queue and a cleaner handoff into human, device, professional, and transactional validation.

Closed milestone

AeroVista Operates · 0.5.0 accepted

Slice C and the operator UI are accepted. AVCC Registry/status/audit persistence and safety behavior were proven; recovery evidence was retained.

Why it matters: governed operational visibility replaces remembered commands as the normal interface.
Architecture converged

Identity · repeatable lanes

AV Account is the human doorway, Identity Gateway / AVCC is authority, App Adapter is the user-facing broker lane, and Connector Kit is the machine/service lane.

Why it matters: future apps can inherit identity instead of rebuilding signup and access control.
Foundation hardened

Signal · identity becomes the work

Registry validation, branch governance, automatic featured selection, production smoke, incident routing, and recovery proof are complete foundation milestones.

Why it matters: Signal can become a role-aware information layer without creating another auth store.
Boundary crossed

LotScope / Pondy · off-code proof next

Pondy Design 4 repository/package QA is complete. LotScope's candidate lifecycle and revalidation capabilities remain active product work.

Why it matters: Pondy's uncertainty is now professional/site/AHJ validation, not repository correctness.
Commerce operational

Cindy Santi · checkout path closed

The five-product collection, 36 sellable size variations, server-authoritative $68 pricing, CORS/preflight, Square checkout, and Printful mappings are established.

Why it matters: remaining work is production-art governance, not rebuilding payments.
Memory became product

Handoff Centralized · live front door

Governed intake reconciled new evidence and returned the inbox to README-only. The Control Center presents canonical Markdown as a private, fail-closed operating surface.

Why it matters: continuity becomes browsable instead of depending on conversation history or one person's memory.

03 · Keep the queue honest

Closed work should stop making AeroVista look unfinished.

Operates 0.5.0 acceptance, Signal's branch/smoke/incident foundation, Pondy Design 4 repository QA, Cindy checkout/CORS/Square/Printful integration, AVCC/ACOS recovery as a recovery project, the NXCore startup-hardening baseline, and intentionally paused Art Localized AVCC machine integration belong in history—not active WIP.

Good portfolio management is not only knowing what remains open. It is refusing to keep solved problems artificially alive.

Conversation-stage ideas remain visible without competing with P0/P1 execution. Promotion should require a defined name, owner, repository or canonical artifact, current boundary, and next gate.

05 · Portfolio risks

The risk profile is changing too.

Stale work appearing active

Completed gates that remain visible distort portfolio health and invite repeated effort.

Engineering past the actual gate

Aleah, RydeSync, Pondy, and B Street now require people, devices, professionals, agencies, or transactions—not simply more code.

Source/runtime/acceptance flattening

Source ahead of runtime is normal. The failure would be pretending one version number proves every layer.

Project sprawl

Exploration can stay inexpensive until a concept earns promotion into governed WIP.

06 · Why the architecture matters

The portfolio is beginning to compound.

AeroVista increasingly behaves like a platform instead of a set of unrelated applications. Each common layer should reduce the friction and cost of the next employee, client, storefront, application, and decision.

Week-end conclusion

The foundation increasingly works. Now prove that it repeats.

The near-term sequence is intentionally narrow: AV Account and Profile Contract → Aleah real-user onboarding → controlled Commerce rollout → Pondy professional validation → Signal identity consumption → Cindy production-art closeout → RydeSync real-device acceptance, with Work Log recovery and Handoff Centralized governance running in parallel.

This is the transition from building AeroVista projects to building the AeroVista operating system.
