From the Founder

Momentum, memory, and operational leverage

This week, AeroVista got smarter.

Over the past several years, AeroVista has generated thousands of conversations, designs, procedures, technical discoveries, project plans, and hard-earned lessons. Until now, too much of that knowledge lived across disconnected systems and scattered workstreams.

This week, that changed. The AeroVista Memory Vault is now live inside the AeroVista Command Center. More than four thousand knowledge artifacts are indexed, published, and accessible through a unified operational interface.

For shareholders, this is more than a technical deployment. It is a step toward building a company that retains what it learns. The goal is simple: stop losing context, reduce repeated work, accelerate onboarding, and create a foundation for AI-assisted operations that improves over time.

The Great Convergence

Energy, compute, money, and data are becoming one strategic system.

The old model treated energy, finance, technology, and information as separate industries. The new model is an integrated stack: electricity powers compute, compute creates data, data drives financial systems, and capital funds infrastructure expansion.

Core Thesis

The system is converging

Sovereign infrastructure is becoming the operating system of global power.

AI does not scale without electricity. Data centers do not operate without grid capacity. Financial settlement is becoming programmable. Governments are treating technology as national security. The winners of the next decade will be the organizations that understand the stack as one connected system.
