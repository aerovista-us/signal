A weekend-to-date briefing: restoring core systems, stabilizing the operating layer, and turning a major setback into stronger doctrine.

Verified Signal · EOW

Weekend ending Sunday, June 28, 2026

Signal Summary

This weekend became an important resilience weekend for AeroVista. Instead of a normal forward-build cycle, an estimated 14–22 focused hours were redirected into restoring, stabilizing, and verifying core operating systems after a serious infrastructure setback.

Core takeaway: recover cleanly, verify honestly, document aggressively, deploy safely, and protect the work.

What Changed

  • AVCC: Restored and verified — container healthy, service health confirmed, public access routed through Cloudflare Access.
  • Core Registry: Moved from failure state into controlled recovery and evidence gathering.
  • Art Localized API: Preserved from the live environment; production configuration recovered from running services.
  • Deployment discipline: A dangerous deployment pattern was identified and is being converted into operating doctrine.

The Business Meaning

The weekend cost forward velocity — roughly one to two concentrated workdays diverted from new build work. The return is also clear: critical services were restored, recovery paths were proven, weak points were identified, and stronger operating rules emerged.

AeroVista is still moving forward. The difference after this weekend is a better understanding of how to protect what is being built.
