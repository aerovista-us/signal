Signal Summary

The EOD note is the daily working record for AeroVista. It should capture visible progress, important edits, unresolved review items, and the next practical moves without turning activity into inflated claims.

What Moved Today

Use this section for shipped pages, updated trainings, cleaned links, published notes, customer-facing copy changes, or operational improvements that are ready for review.

Needs Review

Call out copy that needs approval, visual QA, link checks, rights or attribution questions, stakeholder language, and anything that should not be considered final yet.

Risk Watch

Track fragmentation, overclaiming, stale links, unclear ownership, missing proof, and public-facing language that could imply revenue, delivery capacity, or commitments before verification.

Next Moves

End each EOD with the next three to five moves. Keep them concrete enough that tomorrow starts with direction instead of rediscovery.
