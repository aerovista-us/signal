Weekend ending Sunday, June 28, 2026

Signal Summary

AeroVista redirected an estimated 14–22 focused hours from forward build work into recovery, verification, and stabilization after a serious infrastructure setback. AVCC was restored, Core Registry entered controlled recovery, and Art Localized API configuration was preserved from the live environment.

The strongest signal is resilience under pressure — and the doctrine that source-of-truth discipline now matters as much as feature velocity.

Core takeaway: recover cleanly, verify honestly, document aggressively, deploy safely, and protect the work.

What Changed This Weekend

  • Friday — Recovery mission: AVCC restored and verified; Core Registry shifted from mystery failure into controlled recovery.
  • Saturday — Stabilization: Consolidation work made clear that connected services, containers, and deployment paths need stronger protection.
  • Sunday — Resilience: Art Localized became the next recovery lane; live API state became evidence for reconstruction.
  • Operating doctrine: A dangerous deployment pattern was identified and is being converted into repeatable rules.

The Business Meaning

The weekend cost forward velocity but did not stop momentum. Critical services were restored, recovery paths were proven, and weak points are clearer than before the setback.
