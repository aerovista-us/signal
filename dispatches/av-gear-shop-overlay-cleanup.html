AV Gear Shop Overlay Cleanup

Signal Summary

The AV Gear Shop overlay cleanup reduces drift and clarifies the division of responsibility between the storefront presentation layer and Square checkout data.

Core Rule

The site presents the story, product context, featured order, and creative layout. Square remains the source of truth for checkout, variation IDs, and final pricing.

What Improved

Placeholder descriptions were removed, stale variation references were pruned, featured slots were clarified, and the overlay became easier to audit without conflicting with checkout.

Why It Matters

A storefront is not just a product list. It is a trust surface. Cleaner data rules make it safer to scale products, collections, and campaign pages.

Next Moves

Continue tightening product titles, descriptions, collection logic, and featured presentation while keeping Square as checkout truth.
