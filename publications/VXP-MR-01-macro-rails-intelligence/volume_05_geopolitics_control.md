# Volume V  
## Geopolitics, Control Surfaces & Dollar Power

### 1. Framing

Dollar stablecoins are both **crypto** and **state power instruments**—especially in sanctioned or fragile regimes.

### 2. Key components

- **Iran’s USDT Reserves**
  - ~$500M in Tether as a shadow dollar reserve.
  - Subsequent U.S. sanctions, infrastructure takedown, and kinetic actions.

- **Stablecoins as Shadow Reserves**
  - Use by emerging markets and stressed regimes.
  - Rapid freeze/cutoff capability by issuers and U.S. authorities.

- **Fragmentation Risk**
  - Incentives for alternative rails (non‑USD stablecoins, CBDCs, regional systems).

### 3. Signal cluster: Geo‑Control Cluster

- **Cluster Signals**
  - Stablecoin usage in sanctioned or fragile states.
  - Frequency and speed of freezes/blacklists.
  - Emergence of non‑USD or non‑Western stable rails.

- **Why this cluster matters**
  - Reveals the **true control surface** of “crypto dollars.”
  - Indicates how long the dollar can dominate tokenized rails.
