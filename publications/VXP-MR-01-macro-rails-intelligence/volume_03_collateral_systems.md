# Volume III  
## Collateral Systems & Credit Innovation

### 1. Framing

Crypto is shifting from speculative asset to **collateral and reserve**—especially where it touches housing and institutional credit.

### 2. Key components

- **BTC‑Collateralized Conforming Mortgages**
  - BTC as collateral for 30‑year conforming mortgages.
  - ~250% collateralization, no margin calls.
  - 1% of U.S. mortgages → ~717,000 BTC locked.

- **Institutional Crypto Collateral (JPMorgan)**
  - BTC/ETH as collateral for institutional loans.
  - Normalizes crypto on bank balance sheets.

- **DeFi Reinvestment & Collateral Loops**
  - Tokenized treasuries used as collateral in DeFi.
  - Interaction with CLARITY Act reporting exemptions.

### 3. Signal cluster: BTC Supply Sink & Collateral Cluster

- **Cluster Signals**
  - Volume of BTC‑backed mortgages.
  - BTC/ETH pledged as collateral in institutional programs.
  - Growth of tokenized collateral in DeFi.

- **Why this cluster matters**
  - Creates **structural supply sinks** for BTC.
  - Bridges crypto wealth into real‑world credit channels.
