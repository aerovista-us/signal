# Volume IV  
## Banking, Infrastructure, and Institutional Adoption

### 1. Framing

Banks are not sitting out—they’re **rebuilding their own rails** with tokenization and stablecoins.

### 2. Key components

- **Integrated Fiat/Stablecoin Accounts (Infinite × Erebor)**
  - Single account combining banking, stablecoin mint/burn, and on‑chain transfers.
  - Developer/enterprise infrastructure, not retail UX.

- **Bank‑Issued Stablecoins**
  - JPMorgan, BofA, Citi, Wells building their own stablecoins.
  - Enabled by rules allowing issuance backed by customer cash.

- **OCC Charters & Fed Rail Access**
  - Crypto‑aligned banks with direct access to Fed payment systems.
  - Reduces friction between on‑chain and off‑chain settlement.

### 3. Signal cluster: Institutional Rails Cluster

- **Cluster Signals**
  - Number and scale of bank‑issued stablecoins.
  - Adoption of integrated fiat/on‑chain accounts by enterprises.
  - OCC charter issuance pace.

- **Why this cluster matters**
  - Determines whether **incumbent banks** or **crypto‑native issuers** dominate the new rails.
  - Shapes the competitive landscape for infrastructure builders.
