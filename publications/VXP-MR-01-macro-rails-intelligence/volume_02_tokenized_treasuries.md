# Volume II  
## Tokenized Treasuries & Stablecoin Liquidity Engine

### 1. Framing

Tokenized treasuries and stablecoins are converging into a **parallel liquidity engine** that can amplify or counteract traditional monetary policy.

### 2. Key components

- **Tokenized Treasuries**
  - BlackRock’s tokenized debt fund.
  - Standard Chartered’s $1T tokenization projection.
  - Treasuries as programmable collateral in DeFi and repo.

- **Stablecoin Reserve Dynamics**
  - Tether’s $140B+ in U.S. Treasuries.
  - Bank‑issued stablecoins backed by customer cash.
  - Stablecoin issuers as structural buyers of U.S. debt.

- **Liquidity Multiplication Loop**
  - Deposit → stablecoin → Treasuries → tokenized treasuries → repo → leverage → reinvestment.
  - Multiple yield layers on a single base deposit.

- **BIS Framing: Stablecoins ≈ Rate Cuts**
  - Stablecoin growth acts like a shadow easing mechanism.
  - When aligned with Fed cuts, liquidity can overshoot.

### 3. Signal cluster: Tokenized Collateral Engine Cluster

- **Cluster Signals**
  - AUM in tokenized treasuries.
  - Stablecoin supply growth vs. Fed policy stance.
  - Share of U.S. debt held by stablecoin issuers and tokenized funds.

- **Why this cluster matters**
  - Defines the **real liquidity backdrop** beyond headline rates.
  - Determines how much “hidden easing” is happening via on‑chain rails.
