# Volume VI  
## AI‑Energy Infrastructure & Physical Rails

### 1. Framing

The future of tokenized finance runs on **compute and power**—AI data centers and energy grids are the physical substrate.

### 2. Key components

- **AI Data Centers as Compute‑Energy Systems**
  - Treated as integrated power + compute assets.
  - Co‑located with cheap or strategic energy sources.

- **Energy Markets & Grid Constraints**
  - AI demand reshaping grid planning, LNG flows, and sovereign energy policy.
  - Overlap with financial infrastructure build‑out.

- **Convergence with Tokenized Rails**
  - Same actors often invest in AI infra, energy, and tokenization.
  - Future hubs of liquidity where compute, power, and rails intersect.

### 3. Signal cluster: Physical Rail Cluster

- **Cluster Signals**
  - Location and scale of AI‑grade data centers.
  - New energy capacity tied to compute.
  - Financial products explicitly linked to compute/energy infra.

- **Why this cluster matters**
  - Identifies **geographic and physical choke points** for the internet of money.
  - Guides long‑term strategic positioning.
